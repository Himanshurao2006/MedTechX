NLP & ML Architecture

  • • Preprocessing: Phonetic Hinglish transliteration, stop-word removal, and negation tagging.
  • • Feature Extraction: TF-IDF n-grams + clinical embeddings trained on medical consultations.
  • • Classifier: Ensemble Logistic Regression & Random Forest for 3-class urgency scoring.
  • • Red-Flag Override: Hard-coded clinical rules immediately force High Urgency if acute red flags occur.
